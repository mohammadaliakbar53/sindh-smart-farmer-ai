# 🌱 Sindh Smart Farmer AI

**An AI-powered farming assistant designed to help farmers in Sindh, Pakistan, understand crop problems and access practical agricultural guidance.**

## 📌 About the Project

Sindh Smart Farmer AI is a web application designed to make agricultural guidance more accessible to farmers. Farmers may struggle to identify crop problems, understand plant symptoms, and access useful information in their preferred language.

This project aims to address these challenges through artificial intelligence, image analysis, and an easy-to-use interface.

Developed as a project for the **DITE Hackathon 2026–27**.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Farmers often face difficulties identifying possible crop diseases and understanding what steps to take when their plants show signs of damage. Limited access to understandable agricultural information can make these problems harder to manage.

## 💡 Our Solution

Sindh Smart Farmer AI aims to provide an accessible digital assistant that helps farmers explore possible crop problems and learn about appropriate next steps using AI-generated guidance.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🌿 **AI Crop Health Analysis** — Upload a crop or leaf image to explore possible causes of visible symptoms.
- 🤖 **AI Farming Assistant** — Ask agricultural questions and receive AI-generated guidance.
- 🌐 **Language Accessibility** — Designed to support accessible farming information, including Urdu and English where available.
- 📋 **Practical Recommendations** — Get suggested inspection steps, prevention guidance, and advice on when to consult an agricultural expert.
- 📄 **Farming Reports** — Organize crop observations and AI-generated findings into a useful summary, where supported.
- 📱 **Responsive Interface** — Designed for convenient use on desktop and mobile devices.

*Feature availability depends on the implemented application version and its configured AI services.*

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the deployed website.
2. Navigate to the crop health checker or AI farming assistant.
3. Upload a crop image or describe the problem, if image analysis is available.
4. Enter relevant details, such as the crop name and visible symptoms.
5. Review the AI-generated response and suggested next steps.
6. Consult a qualified agricultural expert for serious or uncertain crop problems.

## 🌾 Example Use Case

A farmer notices yellowing leaves and small brown spots on cotton plants.

Using Sindh Smart Farmer AI, the farmer can describe the symptoms or upload a photo to explore possible causes, learn what to inspect next, and receive general prevention guidance.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Sindh Smart Farmer AI is an informational tool, not a substitute for professional agricultural diagnosis. AI-generated suggestions may be incomplete or incorrect. Crop problems can have multiple causes, and image-based analysis cannot guarantee an accurate diagnosis.

Always verify important decisions with a qualified agricultural expert. Do not apply pesticides or other chemicals solely on the basis of AI-generated recommendations.
